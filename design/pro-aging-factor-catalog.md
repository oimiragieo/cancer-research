## Circulating EV / sEV lane (Ralph wake #13)
- Young plasma **sEVs** (Nat Aging 2024): lifespan + multi-tissue rejuvenation via miR-144/149/455 → PGC-1α; aged sEVs carry opposing miRNAs.
- Young **CDC-EVs** (Sci Rep 2023): multi-organ + lifespan in rats; EV fraction necessary for young-blood effect in vitro.
- Translational preference: defined miRNA/protein cargo or GMP cell-derived EV products over raw young-plasma transfusion (same selectivity principle as GDF11/PF4).



## Neutralization clinical lane (Ralph wake #15)
- **CCL11/CCR3**: AKST4290 (lazucirnon) oral CCR3 antagonist — Phase 2 PD done (unpublished); AMD/retinopathy ongoing. Anti-CCL11 Ab mimics environmental enrichment cognition benefits (Scabia 2021).
- Strategy remains selective neutralization/mimetics (Bieri Nat Neurosci 2023) over plasma transfusion.

## Plasma dilution vs young plasma (Ralph wake #16)
- **TPE/albumin replacement** (Fuentealba Aging Cell 2025; Conboy 2022): removes pro-aging factors; TPE+IVIG strongest clock signal (~2.6y). Prefer dilution/neutralization over Ambrosia-style young-donor plasma transfusion.



## Klotho → PF4 cognition axis (Ralph wake #22)
- Systemic **low-dose klotho** enhances aged NHP cognition (Castner Nat Aging 2023); high dose fails — narrow therapeutic window.
- **PF4** is a sufficient platelet messenger of klotho cognition (Park 2023); prefer defined protein/mimetic over unregulated gene-therapy pilots.
