# Gap dossier — TNBC neoantigen vaccine

**Status:** DEFERRED WITH EVIDENCE (climb)
**Updated:** 2026-09-05

## Claim under test

Personalized neoantigen vaccines ± CPI produce durable treatment-free remission in high-risk or metastatic TNBC.

## Evidence banked

- Nature 2025 Ph1 adjuvant n=14: durable T-cell responses; 11 relapse-free up to 6y; 3 recurred
- KEYNOTE-522: pembro + chemo improves pCR/EFS in early TNBC (surrogate-heavy)
- NCT06324240: early personalized vaccine + CPI — efficacy unproven

## Negatives

- No randomized metastatic TNBC neoantigen vaccine OS win banked
- pCR ≠ cure
