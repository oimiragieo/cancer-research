# Gap dossier: Senolytic CAR-T human scalability
**Status:** DEFERRED WITH EVIDENCE (2026-08-20)  
**Verdict:** uPAR senolytic CAR-T is **strong in mice** (including aged + prophylactic single-dose persistence) but **no published human aging CAR-T efficacy trial** yet. Clinical path = disease indication first (fibrosis/cancer), then geriatrics.

## Evidence
| Source | Finding |
|--------|---------|
| Amor et al. Nature 2020 | uPAR CAR-T clears senescent cells; improves lung cancer combo Rx + liver fibrosis in mice; CRS-like toxicity at supra-therapeutic dose |
| Amor et al. Nat Aging 2024 | Single dose improves aged metabolic fitness; prophylactic benefit lasting months–>1 year in mice |
| Amor Lab / reviews | Optimization ongoing; goal = clinical development; off-tumor uPAR (bronchial epithelium, myeloid subsets) flagged |
| NRMCB 2024 comment | Must confirm efficacy/safety in older humans with compromised immunity before aging indication |

## Design implications (ultra-design L3)
1. Senolytic CAR-T is the **leading Pathway-A clearance modality** for persistence vs intermittent small molecules.
2. Human entry: fibrosis or oncology senescence-combo, with suPAR as PD biomarker; safety switches mandatory.
3. Do not assume young-adult CAR-T cancer playbooks transfer to frail elderly without dedicated trials.

## Remaining unknown
Human MTD, on-target off-tumor toxicity, manufacturing scale for elective aging use, antigen redundancy beyond uPAR.
