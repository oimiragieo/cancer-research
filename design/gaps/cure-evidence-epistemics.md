## Laws

1. **COMPLETED ≠ Results** on ClinicalTrials.gov
2. **MRD/CR/pCR ≠ cured** — store assay, sensitivity, timepoint, denominator
3. **Adjacency ≠ invention** — sister tumor / label mg never fills unpublished arm
4. **Press ≠ peer review** — bank medium until tables
5. **Single-arm time-to-event** — weak for comparative cure claims
6. **Name collisions** — verify NCT and drug identity
7. **Absence = searched as of DATE** — not universal nonexistence

## Cure-fraction language

Reserve **cure** for durable disease-free survival / treatment-free remission with adequate follow-up. Prefer **cure-adjacent**, **durable remission**, **survival plateau** otherwise.
