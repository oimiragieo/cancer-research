# Gap dossier: NAD precursors & human healthspan
**Status:** DEFERRED WITH EVIDENCE (2026-08-20)  
**Verdict:** Oral NR/NMN **engage target** (raise NAD metabolites) and are generally tolerable; **causal healthspan benefit in humans is NOT established**. Rodent ≠ human.

## Evidence
| Source | Finding |
|--------|---------|
| Nat Metab 2025 review (Vinten et al.) | Age-related NAD decline in humans sparsely replicated; precursor trials show limited efficacy; need tissue-specific clinical maps |
| PRISMA review 2026 (ARR) | 33 human interventions: biomarkers up; metabolic/vascular/performance outcomes mixed/null; no eligible IV NAD anti-aging outcome trials |
| PMC10692436 (2023) | Small n (often ≤40), heterogeneous endpoints; safety OK; physiology unclear |
| Disease-context RCTs | NR in PD, PAD (NICE), COPD, MCI, Werner — endpoint-specific, not general healthspan |

## Design implications (ultra-design L2)
1. NAD repletion is a **supportive cosubstrate lever**, not a proven immortality drug.
2. Prefer trials with **prespecified clinical endpoints** + tissue NAD measures over wellness biomarker studies.
3. Stack position: optional L2 adjuvant after mTOR/senescence/clone gates — never the sole Pathway-A strategy.

## Remaining unknown
Which tissues actually rise NAD after oral precursors in older humans; whether CD38/PARP sinks negate benefit; long outcome RCTs.
