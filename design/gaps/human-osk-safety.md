# Gap dossier: Human OSK safety windows
**Status:** DEFERRED WITH EVIDENCE (2026-08-20)  
**Verdict:** First-in-human OSK has begun as **tissue-restricted, inducible AAV-OSK** in the eye — not systemic immortality therapy. Safety window = local delivery + doxycycline on-switch + short activation.

## Evidence
| Source | Finding |
|--------|---------|
| Life Biosciences ER-100 NCT07290244 | Phase 1 recruiting: intravitreal AAV delivering OSK (OCT4/SOX2/KLF4), doxycycline 8 weeks to activate; OAG then NAION cohorts; n≈18 |
| MIT Tech Review / SciAm 2026 | FDA cleared FIH; first participant treated (June 2026 report); eye chosen as contained organ (“one left if it goes wrong”) |
| Preclinical (Lu 2020 OSK vision; Ocampo pulsed OSKM) | Pulsed/local OSK can restore function; continuous systemic OSKM lethal/teratoma risk in mice |

## Design implications (ultra-design L4)
1. Prefer **tissue-restricted + inducible** delivery over systemic continuous OSKM.
2. Human safety signal will come from ocular Phase 1 (inflammation, tumorigenesis, identity loss markers) before any multi-organ claim.
3. Whole-body OSK remains **explicitly out of near-term stack** until multi-organ FIH exists.

## Remaining unknown (not closable by literature alone)
Long-term oncogenicity, switch immunogenicity (E. coli/herpes components), and translation beyond retina.
