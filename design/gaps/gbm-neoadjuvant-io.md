# Gap dossier — GBM neoadjuvant IO

**Status:** DEFERRED WITH EVIDENCE (climb)
**Updated:** 2026-09-05

## Claim under test

Neoadjuvant multi-checkpoint blockade while tumor is in situ improves durable OS in newly diagnosed IDH-wt, MGMT-unmethylated GBM vs standard Stupp-era care.

## Evidence banked

- Nature Medicine 2025 neoadjuvant triplet case-level signal (nivo+ipi+relatlimab) — case ≠ Ph3
- **GIANT NCT06816927** (Duke): Ph2 RECRUITING; perioperative **nivolumab +/- relatlimab** (not ipi triplet); primary endpoints feasibility + TIL biology; OS/PFS descriptive only; Results absent

## Negatives

- No randomized OS win for neoadjuvant multi-CPI in IDH-wt GBM as of wake #2
- **Name/protocol honesty:** do not equate Nature Med case triplet with GIANT nivo+/-relatlimab arms
- Steroid exposure and immune privilege remain load-bearing confounders
